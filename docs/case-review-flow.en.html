Case Review & Processing Workflow

KOI Recall Platform Admin · A one-page overview for operations & compliance teams
System / Consumer Customer Service Compliance Logistics Finance
submitted Consumer / System

① Consumer submits a claim

The consumer submits a claim on the KOI website; the system automatically records the requested remedy (replacement or refund).

intake_review Customer Service

② Intake check

Check that the submission is complete and ready to move into triage.

triage Customer Service

③ Triage & verification

Verify product details and evidence; resolve any product or evidence anomalies.

If a safety incident is involved
⚠ Compliance goes first: reportability review
If the case involves a safety incident, Compliance must first complete the "does this need to be reported to CPSC?" review; the customer-service flow pauses here until it is done.
case_review Customer Service

④ Case review

Complete the product, evidence, and risk assessment; decide whether the claim qualifies.

Awaiting info
✉ Need more info from the consumer?
Put the case into "need info" and notify the consumer; once the materials arrive, return to Case review.
Not qualified
If the claim doesn't qualify you can reject it; you can also mark it a duplicate, or the consumer may withdraw — all three are final states.
resolution_approval Customer Service

⑤ Approve the remedy

Once the claim is approved, sign off the remedy for the consumer: replacement or refund.

▶
replacement_processing Logistics

⑥A Ship the replacement

Logistics arranges the shipment in the external fulfillment system.

▶
refund_processing Finance

⑥B Issue the refund

Finance completes the payout via Refund Export in the external system.

Compliance⑦ Closure review closure_review

  • The remedy must be fully completed externally (shipped / paid) — otherwise this step cannot be entered
  • If a safety incident was involved, the reportability review must be concluded (filed, or confirmed non-reportable)

Both gates must pass before the case can close; if issues are found in review, send it back to Case review.

completed ✔ Flow complete

⑧ Case closed

The consumer sees "Completed" on the website; every action leaves an audit trail.

✔ Closedclosed · Remedy done & review passed
✖ Rejectedrejected · Claim did not qualify
⧉ Duplicateduplicate · Same as another case
↩ Withdrawnwithdrawn · Consumer withdrew
The small gray codes are internal status IDs, for mapping to the admin UI / consumer lookup page.
