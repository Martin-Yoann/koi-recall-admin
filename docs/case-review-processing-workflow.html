Case Review & Processing Workflow

KOI Recall Platform Admin · A one-page overview for operations & compliance teams · revised to match the shipped implementation (2026-09-11)
System / Consumer Customer Service Compliance Logistics Finance ✉ = a consumer email is queued at this step
Consumer / Systemsubmitted

①Consumer submits a claim

The consumer submits a claim on the KOI website; the system automatically records the requested remedy (replacement or refund) and queues the confirmation email.

✉ claim confirmation
▼
Customer Serviceintake_review

②Intake check

Check that the submission is complete and ready to move into triage. (The case is still in status submitted; “Intake review” is its current stage.)

▼
Customer Servicetriage

③Triage & verification

Verify product details and evidence; resolve any product or evidence anomalies.

If a safety incident is involved

⚠ Compliance goes first: reportability review

While the “Does this need to be reported to the CPSC?” review is pending, the case’s current stage switches to Compliance review (compliance_review) and it is tracked in the urgent-injury SLA queue (4 h). Customer service may still move the case forward — the enforced block is at Closure review (Gate 2), which cannot be passed until the review is concluded.

▼
Customer Servicecase_review

④Case review

Complete the product, evidence, and risk assessment; decide whether the claim qualifies.

Awaiting info

✉ Need more info from the consumer?

Put the case into “need info” (stage awaiting_consumer_information) — the consumer is emailed with the requested materials; once they arrive, return to Case review.

Not qualified

✉ Reject · duplicate · withdraw

If the claim doesn’t qualify you can reject it; you can also mark it a duplicate, or the consumer may withdraw — all three are final states. A written reason (≥10 chars) is required for every role and is included in the consumer email.

▼
Customer Serviceresolution_approval

⑤Approve the remedy

Once the claim is approved, sign off the remedy for the consumer: replacement or refund.

✉ replacement approved / refund approved
▼
Logisticsreplacement_processing

⑥A Ship the replacement

Logistics arranges the shipment in the external fulfillment system.

✉ replacement shipped
Financerefund_processing

⑥B Issue the refund

Finance completes the payout via Refund Export in the external system.

✉ refund completed
▼

Compliance ⑦ Closure review closure_review

◍The remedy must be fully completed externally (shipped / paid) — otherwise this step cannot be entered.
◍If a safety incident was involved, the reportability review must be concluded (filed, or confirmed non-reportable).

Both gates must pass before the case can close; if issues are found in review, send it back to Case review.

▼
✓ Flow completecompleted

⑧Case closed

The consumer sees “Completed” on the website; every action leaves an audit trail. (A force-closed case without a completed remedy shows “Closed” instead and queues a case-closure email.)

✉ case completed / case closed
✔ Closedclosed · Remedy done & review passed
✖ Rejectedrejected · Claim did not qualify
⧉ Duplicateduplicate · Same as another case
↩ Withdrawnwithdrawn · Consumer withdrew
The small gray codes are workflow stage IDs — they map to “Current stage” in the admin UI. Internal case statuses are a separate set:
submitted · triage · under_review · need_info · approved · closure_review · closed · rejected · duplicate · withdrawn
The department on each stage is informational; the system roles are ADMIN and MANAGER only.
